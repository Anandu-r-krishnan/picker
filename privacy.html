Privacy policy

Storycraft Client Picks · last updated 11 October 2026

Storycraft uses this page to let clients choose photos and videos from their shoot for editing. This policy explains what information is used and why.

What we collect

  • Your Google account email and name, only if your gallery is private and you sign in with Google. We use it to check that the gallery was shared with you.
  • What you send us: the photos and videos you pick, your notes, your name and the phone number you enter.

How we use it

Only to show you your gallery and to prepare your edit. Your picks are saved to Storycraft’s own Google Drive and Google Sheets, and Storycraft receives an email with them. We don’t sell or share your information, and we don’t use it for advertising.

What stays on your device

Your picks are also remembered in your browser, so you can close the page and come back. Clearing your browser data removes them.

How long we keep it

As long as needed to finish and deliver your project. Ask us any time and we’ll delete your information.

Services we use

Google (sign-in, Drive, Sheets and Gmail) and GitHub Pages (which hosts this page). Their own privacy policies apply to how they handle data.

Contact

Questions or deletion requests: storycraftcreatives@gmail.com
